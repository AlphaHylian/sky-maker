Sky Maker

Turns one picture into a Minecraft 1.8.9 custom sky (MCPatcher / OptiFine).

Process credit: @banjeerl on YouTube

1. Upload

Everything runs in your browser. Your picture is never uploaded.

2. Fix problems

Tick a problem to apply its automatic fix. Then fine-tune it with the controls. Ctrl+Z undoes the last change.
